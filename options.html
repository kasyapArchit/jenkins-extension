Jenkins Launcher

Point the extension at your Jenkins controller once, then star pipelines from the popup.

Connection
No trailing path. This is the host your VPN reaches.
API token skips Jenkins CSRF checks. Session mode needs you logged in to Jenkins in this browser and fetches a crumb per build.
Create one at /me/security on your Jenkins. Stored in this profile's extension storage, unencrypted.
Status tracking
Chrome will not fire alarms faster than every 30 seconds.
Getting a token
  1. Open Jenkins, click your name, then Security.
  2. Add new token, name it "chrome extension", copy the value.
  3. Paste it above with your Jenkins user ID and save.
